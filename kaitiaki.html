Meet the team

Kaitiaki Hamon

Landscaper

Kaitiaki brings eight years of hands-on landscaping experience to Asgard's Gardens, most of it working side by side with Richard — first at Fourways Grading, then at Garden Magic Landscaping. He's a sod specialist by trade: fast, precise, and known for laying clean, tight sod at a pace most crews can't match, including commercial installations and new-development boulevards.

On site, Kaitiaki is the detail guy — crisp edges, clean grades, and finishes that hold up. He works the same way he expects from a crew: no shortcuts, no rushed jobs, and everything left better than it was found.

For Kaitiaki, good landscaping is honest work done right the first time.

Beyond landscaping, Kaitiaki is a visual artist and musician — explore his portfolio.
