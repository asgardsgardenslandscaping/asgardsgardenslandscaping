Landscaping Services

One-time landscape construction for Edmonton homes — designed around clay soil, freeze–thaw cycles, and a short growing season. No mowing contracts, no shortcuts.

Service 01

Grading & Drainage

Everything starts with water management. Edmonton's clay-heavy soil holds water instead of draining it, and spring melt arrives fast. We grade your lot so water moves away from your foundation — not toward it — and fix low spots, pooling, and soggy corners for good.

  • Final grade and lot leveling around foundations
  • Swales and surface drainage that handle spring melt
  • Downspout extensions and runoff redirection

Get a quote for grading

Service 02

Sod Installation

Nothing transforms a yard faster than fresh sod — but only if the ground underneath is right. We strip, level, and amend the soil before a single roll goes down, lay tight seams with staggered joints, and roll everything flat so you get an even lawn that roots properly instead of lifting at the edges.

  • Soil prep: stripping, levelling, and amending Edmonton's clay base
  • Tight, staggered seams rolled flat — no gaps, no ridges
  • Watering and care guidance for the first critical weeks

Get a quote for sod

Service 03

Decorative Rock & Mulch

Want a yard that looks sharp with almost no upkeep? River rock, rundle stone, and mulch beds are the answer — especially along foundations and in hard-to-mow spots. We lay proper landscape fabric, crisp edging, and the right depth of material so weeds stay down and the stone stays put.

  • River rock, rundle, and decorative stone in a range of colours
  • Mulch beds with clean metal or stone edging
  • Weed barrier done right — fabric under rock, not on top of soil

Get a quote for rock & mulch

Service 04

Walkways & Pathways

A walkway should still be level after its third winter. We build paver and natural-stone paths on compacted granular bases with proper edge restraint — the boring, invisible work that keeps stones from heaving, sinking, and wobbling through Edmonton's freeze–thaw.

  • Paver and flagstone walkways on compacted frost-resistant bases
  • Polymeric sand joints and solid edge restraint
  • Designs that fit your home's style and your yard's flow

Get a quote for walkways

Service 05

Garden Beds

Edmonton is Zone 3 — short summers, long winters, and plants that have to earn their place. We build defined beds with quality amended soil, hardy plantings that actually survive here, and edging that keeps mulch and soil where they belong instead of creeping across your lawn.

  • Defined bed shapes with clean, lasting edging
  • Quality soil blends amended for clay conditions
  • Hardy Zone 3 plantings suited to real Edmonton weather

Get a quote for garden beds

Service 06

Retaining Walls

Sloped yard? Eroding bank? A retaining wall turns unusable slope into level, useful space — but only if it's built to hold. We compact proper bases, use the right block and drainage behind the wall, and finish clean so it looks as good as it performs through every freeze and thaw.

  • Block and natural-stone walls built with drainage behind them
  • Compacted bases engineered for frost movement
  • Terraced designs that reclaim sloped yards

Get a quote for retaining walls

Service 07

Water Features

There's nothing like the sound of moving water in your own backyard. We build ponds, bubbling rocks, and rock water features sized for your space — with proper liners, filtration, and winterization guidance so your feature survives Edmonton winters year after year.

  • Ponds, bubblers, and rock waterfalls
  • Proper liners, pumps, and filtration from day one
  • Winterization guidance for harsh Alberta winters

Get a quote for water features
