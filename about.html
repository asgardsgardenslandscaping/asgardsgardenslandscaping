Our story

Built on long days and high standards

Asgard's Gardens Landscaping was started by two guys who genuinely enjoy working outdoors and take pride in doing things the right way.

Richard and Craig worked together for years at Fourways Grading and Garden Magic Landscaping (now owned by Park Landscaping). They spent a lot of long days side by side, learning every part of landscaping hands-on — from grading and sod to hardscapes and water features. Over time, they realized they weren't just learning how to landscape; they were learning how jobs should be done… and how often they aren't.

Like a lot of people in this trade, they were working hard, breaking their backs, and helping build other companies while putting their own ideas and standards on hold. Eventually, they decided it made more sense to put that effort into something they could stand behind and be proud of.

So they started Asgard's Gardens Landscaping.

Our promise

The crew you call is the crew you get

This is Richard's company, and both of us are on the ground doing the work together on every job. When you call, meet for a quote, or see work happening on your property — it's us. No rotating crews, no strangers showing up halfway through a project.
